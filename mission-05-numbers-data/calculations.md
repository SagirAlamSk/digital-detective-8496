# Task 5.1 — Manual Number Conversions

### 1. Decimal 13 to binary

13 ÷ 2 = 6 remainder 1
6 ÷ 2 = 3 remainder 0
3 ÷ 2 = 1 remainder 1
1 ÷ 2 = 0 remainder 1

Reading the remainders from bottom to top:
13₁₀ = 1101₂

### 2. Decimal 25 to binary

25 ÷ 2 = 12 remainder 1
12 ÷ 2 = 6 remainder 0
6 ÷ 2 = 3 remainder 0
3 ÷ 2 = 1 remainder 1
1 ÷ 2 = 0 remainder 1

25₁₀ = 11001₂

### 3. Binary 1010 to decimal

(1 × 2³) + (0 × 2²) + (1 × 2¹) + (0 × 2⁰)
= 8 + 0 + 2 + 0
= 10

1010₂ = 10₁₀

### 4. Binary 1101 to decimal

(1 × 2³) + (1 × 2²) + (0 × 2¹) + (1 × 2⁰)
= 8 + 4 + 0 + 1
= 13

1101₂ = 13₁₀

### 5. Hexadecimal A to decimal

A represents 10 in hexadecimal.

A₁₆ = 10₁₀

### 6. Hexadecimal 1F to decimal

(1 × 16¹) + (15 × 16⁰)
= 16 + 15
= 31

1F₁₆ = 31₁₀

### 7. Binary 1111 to hexadecimal

1111₂ = 8 + 4 + 2 + 1
= 15

15 in hexadecimal is F.

1111₂ = F₁₆
